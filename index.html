A thickened or discolored nail with cracks could be fungus, but there are additional causes for this, too. Using a surface-applied nail-care formula to defer appropriate assessment for a continuing or deteriorating problem that needs clinical attention may leave the underlying diagnosis unidentified.

Also, some people may have skin irritation or sensitivity to botanical ingredients applied externally. Burning, itching, dryness, redness, or contact dermatitis may be among the reactions. Those with known sensitivities should review the ingredient label and stop use if significant irritation develops. Avoiding contact with eyes and broken skin is also sensible, unless the product packaging information specifically specifies an exception.

Another drawback is the evidence base. Customer feedback or manufacturer descriptions cannot serve instead of clinical research under carefully controlled settings. If KeraFirm has not been tested in clinical trials for a specific nail condition, treatment or eradication claims should be regarded critically.
